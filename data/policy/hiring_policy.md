# Larkfield Labs hiring policy for candidate replies

Larkfield Labs is a fictional company. This policy is synthetic and exists so
that drafted replies have one written source of facts. A reply may state only
what this file contains.

## Hiring stages

1. Application review
2. Take-home task (5 days)
3. People team call (20 minutes)
4. Paid trial (3 days)
5. Offer

## Status updates

- Applications are reviewed within 5 business days of submission.
- Take-home submissions are reviewed within 5 business days.
- A reply must not state or hint at an outcome. Only a recruiter shares
  outcomes.
- A reply must not state which stage a named candidate is at unless the stage
  was supplied with the message by the hiring system.

## Rescheduling

- A candidate may reschedule a call once, with at least 24 hours' notice.
- Rescheduling is done through the scheduling link in the invitation email.
- A second reschedule request goes to the recruiter.

## Take-home task

- The task runs for 5 days from the moment the candidate accepts it.
- One extension of up to 48 hours is available on request before the deadline.
- If a task link or upload fails, the candidate should reply with the error
  text and a screenshot. The recruiter restores access and the lost time is
  added back.

## Compensation and contracts

- Pay, contract terms and start dates are discussed on the People team call,
  never by email.
- A reply must not quote a figure, a range, a currency amount or a start date.

## Documents

- Documents are uploaded through the candidate portal.
- Candidates must never email identity documents. If one arrives by email, a
  person handles it.

## Feedback

- Written feedback is offered to candidates who completed the take-home task
  or a later stage. It is sent within 10 business days of the request.
- Candidates who left at application review do not receive individual feedback.

## Accommodations

- Requests for adjustments are handled by the People team, who reply within 2
  business days.
- A reply may acknowledge the request and say who will respond. It must not
  grant, refuse or ask for medical details.

## Withdrawals

- A withdrawal is confirmed in writing.
- Candidate data is kept for 6 months unless the candidate asks for deletion.

## Always handled by a person

- Complaints about staff or about the process.
- Allegations of discrimination or unfair treatment.
- Legal threats or messages from lawyers.
- Data access or deletion requests.
- Messages showing distress or a personal crisis.
- Identity documents sent by email.

## Tone

Plain, warm and brief. 90 words or fewer. No exclamation marks. Sign off as
"Larkfield Labs Hiring Team".
